The glossary

Glossary

Definitions of the biological and computational terms used in these guides.

21 terms shown

TCR · T-cell receptor
A receptor on a T cell. This resource focuses on alpha–beta TCRs that recognize peptides presented by MHC molecules.
Peptide
A short chain of amino acids, often a fragment of a larger protein. It is the sequence scored in the supplied TCR and HLA context.
MHC
Major histocompatibility complex. MHC molecules hold and display peptides at a cell’s surface. Class I generally presents to CD8 T cells; class II generally presents to CD4 T cells.
pMHC
A peptide together with the MHC molecule presenting it. The receptor encounters this combination.
HLA
Human leukocyte antigen: the name used for human MHC. An HLA allele identifies a particular genetic version, such as HLA-B*27:05.
CDR3
A variable region of a T-cell receptor chain. The component API asks for the CDR3 amino-acid sequences of both the alpha and beta chains.
V and J genes
Gene segments used to assemble a receptor. DecoderTCR combines V and J gene names with each CDR3 to reconstruct the receptor chains.
Epitope
The part of an antigen recognized by an immune receptor. In this site’s peptide–MHC setting, it usually refers to the peptide target.
Clonotype
A way to group T cells by shared receptor identity. The exact grouping rule depends on the dataset; read its definition.
PLL · pseudo-log-likelihood
DecoderTCR’s masked-peptide sequence score. Higher means higher model likelihood in the supplied context. It is not a calibrated binding probability.
Zero-shot evaluation
Evaluation without epitope-specific training for the task, as described by the repository. It does not mean every example is absent from pretraining.
Model checkpoint
A saved set of model parameters. Record the exact checkpoint when comparing or reproducing results.
AUROC
A summary of ranking discrimination across thresholds. It is the area under the receiver operating characteristic curve.
AUPRC
A precision–recall summary, useful for retrieval tasks with rare positives. Its interpretation depends on the evaluation-set prevalence.
Recall@K
The fraction of known positive examples recovered among the top K ranked candidates in an evaluation.
IEGR
Iterative Entropy-Guided Refinement: a sequence-generation method that commits low-entropy positions first and refines the sequence in its updated context.
PSSM
Position-specific scoring matrix. A table of amino-acid scores at each sequence position. The current profile tutorials derive their matrices from model-predicted positional preferences; check the workflow for its scoring and background conventions.
Protein language model
A model that learns patterns in amino-acid sequences. A masked model predicts hidden amino acids from the available sequence context.
Fine-tuning
Further training that adapts an existing model to a more specific dataset or task.
Embedding
A numerical representation produced by a model. The sequence API can return embeddings of reconstructed complexes or their regions.
Held-out data
Examples set aside from a particular training process for evaluation. Whether related examples overlap with training still matters.

Biology background: Immunobiology and Molecular Biology of the Cell. Model-specific terms: DecoderTCR documentation.
