Suggest a benchmark

    
A benchmark is a defined test of model performance. To propose one,
    describe the question it would address, the data, and a suitable comparison.

    
    
      
  • Name the task. Describe the decision a scientist would
      make using the result, and which model or resource the test concerns.
    • 
      
  • Describe the data. Link its source and describe the
      receptors, peptides and HLA context that are available. Note any known
      overlap with training data, or say when you do not know.
    • 
      
  • Propose a comparison. Say what you would compare and
      which outcome would answer the question. Keep model scores and
      experimental measurements clearly labeled.
    • 
    

    
Explore the current data and evidence
    before preparing your suggestion.


    
Details to include

    

      For a biological question or an experimental observation
      
Describe what was measured, the relevant experimental context and any
      controls you can share. Separate your observation from your interpretation.
      If you are comparing it with a prediction, identify the model and its inputs.
      Include enough detail to distinguish a model error from a difference in
      experimental conditions.

    

    

      For a notebook or software issue
      
Link the notebook or command, name the model and software version, and
      say whether you used a CPU or GPU. Include the exact error text and the
      smallest public example that shows the problem. Describe what you expected
      and what happened instead.

    

    

      For a clearer explanation or tutorial
      
Link the page and name the step or phrase where you got stuck. Tell us
      what you already knew and what you needed next. You can suggest a revision,
      or simply identify the question the page did not answer.
