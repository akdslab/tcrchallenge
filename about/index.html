About the project

TCR Challenge is a Biohub-led project developing open science tools to decode and design T-cell recognition. The name refers to the scientific challenge of predicting which peptide–MHC targets a T-cell receptor recognizes. We bring together the DecoderTCR sequence model, the DecoderTCR-Fold structure model, and CLASP-seq experiments, with data and practical guides for researchers and anyone curious about the biology.

Funding and support come from Biohub, NIH, University of Chicago, Huang Foundation, and Breakthrough T1D/Lupus Research Alliance/National Multiple Sclerosis Society.
