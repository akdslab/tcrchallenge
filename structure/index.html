About DecoderTCR-Fold

DecoderTCR-Fold is fine-tuned from ESMFold2 base to score TCR–pMHC pairs and predict their complex structures from sequence. Like DecoderTCR, it uses information about the TCR alpha and beta chains, the peptide and the HLA molecule. The structure-model guide defines how those inputs are supplied to the software.
